# FOI request draft: JobSeeker deaths by partial capacity to work

Drafted 7 October 2026. **Pete lodges this himself.** Not sent.

## How to lodge

- [ ] Fill in the reply email below (which inbox you want replies in).
- [ ] Email to **FOI@dss.gov.au**, subject below. Address from search results only; check it on the [DSS FOI page](https://www.dss.gov.au/access-information/freedom-information) before sending.
- [ ] Optional: send it through [Right to Know](https://www.righttoknow.org.au/) instead, so the request and answer are public and linkable from the post. The trade-off: your name and the whole exchange are published.
- [ ] Note the date sent. DSS must acknowledge within 14 days and decide within 30 days (extensions possible).

## Why DSS and why this shape

- DSS already made this table once under section 17: [FOI 2122-068](https://dss.gov.au/system/files/foi-attachments/foi-2122-068-document.pdf) (JobSeeker cancellations due to death, 2019-2021, Services Australia data). Asking for the same extract with one extra column is the smallest possible job.
- A broad request for death rates across all payments since 2003 was refused as too much work ([Right to Know](https://righttoknow.org.au/request/categorised_mortality_rate_for_g)). So: one payment, fixed years, accept their definitions, items that can be dropped separately.
- Denominators (recipients with and without partial capacity each December) are already published by DSS, so they aren't asked for.
- If DSS says Services Australia holds the partial-capacity field, they can transfer it (s16). No need to resend.

## Costs

- No application fee. Charges can apply to non-personal requests: $15 an hour search and retrieval, $20 an hour decision-making after the first 5 hours free (OAIC). The draft asks for charges to be waived on public-interest grounds (s29).
- If they send a charges notice, you have 30 days to pay, contest it, or narrow the request.

## The email

**Subject:** FOI request: JobSeeker cancellations due to death by partial capacity to work

> Dear FOI team,
>
> This is a request under the Freedom of Information Act 1982.
>
> In FOI 2122-068 (released 14 February 2022) the Department created a document under section 17 showing Newstart Allowance/JobSeeker Payment cancellations recorded as due to the death of the recipient, by calendar year, for 2019 to 2021.
>
> I'm asking for a document created the same way, using the same definitions and caveats, covering:
>
> 1. Newstart Allowance/JobSeeker Payment cancellations recorded as due to the death of the recipient, by calendar year from 2014 to 2025, split by whether the recipient had a partial capacity to work recorded at the date of cancellation (yes or no).
> 2. The same counts as item 1, also split by the recipient's age at death: 22-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55 and over.
> 3. The same counts as item 1 for Disability Support Pension, split by whether the person had been paid Newstart Allowance/JobSeeker Payment at any time in the 12 months before death (yes or no).
>
> Each item can be decided on its own. If any one would be too much work, please release the others rather than refusing the whole request. If 2014 to 2018 is hard to produce, 2019 to 2025 is fine. I'm happy to accept small cells suppressed or rounded, and the Department's own definitions.
>
> I ask that any charges be waived in the public interest (section 29). The AIHW report Mortality outcomes among people receiving income support (September 2026) found people on unemployment payments die at about four times the rate of people not on income support, and it's being used in public debate about the JobSeeker rate. These figures show how much of that gap relates to recipients' health. I'll publish the results with analysis on my website.
>
> Please reply to [your email].
>
> Cheers,
> Pete Owen
