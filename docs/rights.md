# Republishing rights (research, 7 October 2026)

Research only, not legal advice. Several sources were seen via search snippets because the
pages returned 403 or timed out; those are marked UNVERIFIED.

## Decision

| What | Risk | Do it? |
|---|---|---|
| Extracted statistics as CSV, linked to source, with "extracted from released document X" | Low: counts are facts; a simple table has thin or no compilation copyright (IceTV v Nine, HCA 2009; Telstra v Phone Directories, FCAFC 2010) | Yes: this is the product |
| Link to disclosure log entry / Right to Know thread | Lowest | Yes |
| Republish full PDFs | Moderate: FOI release grants no licence; agency sites are CC BY 4.0 but disclosure-log coverage unconfirmed; third-party material and personal information excluded | No: keep PDFs as a private working archive |

Rules for the published data:
- Leave out personal information and third-party tables.
- Attribute: "© Commonwealth of Australia", source agency, FOI reference, link.
- Takedown process: remove on request, no argument.

## Findings

- **FOI Act ss 90-91** protect the agency and officers who release documents in good faith, not the person who republishes them. [OAIC](https://oaic.gov.au/freedom-of-information/freedom-of-information-guidance-for-government-agencies/guidance-on-handling-a-freedom-of-information-request/legal-definitions-and-questions/what-protections-does-the-freedom-of-information-act-1982-provide-from-civil-liability-and-breach-of-copyright). s 91 "access is not authorisation of publication" wording: UNVERIFIED against the Act text.
- **OAIC FOI Guidelines Part 14** (disclosure logs): agencies should state reuse terms and use open licences where possible; third-party copyright may need permission; documents are generally removed after 2 years unless of enduring value (National Archives approval; the log entry stays). [Part 14](https://www.oaic.gov.au/freedom-of-information/freedom-of-information-guidance-for-government-agencies/foi-guidelines/part-14-disclosure-log)
- **DSS and Services Australia websites**: CC BY 4.0 except coat of arms, logos, third-party content, trade marks, images. UNVERIFIED wording (search summaries of [DSS](https://dss.gov.au/using-our-website/copyright) and [Services Australia](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/copyright)); whether it covers disclosure-log PDFs is unconfirmed.
- **Commonwealth default**: CC BY for public sector information ([AGD licensing guidelines](https://www.ag.gov.au/rights-and-protections/publications/guidelines-licensing-public-sector-information-australian-government-entities)); any 2024 update UNVERIFIED.
- **Right to Know**: robots.txt allows `*/request/*/response/*/attach/*` but disallows response pages, feeds and search. House rules ban scripts, bots and unapproved automation; commercial use needs Pro ([house rules](https://www.righttoknow.org.au/help/house_rules), snippet only). The JSON endpoints returned a Cloudflare challenge on 7 October 2026. **Automated access paused; permission request drafted in `righttoknow-permission-request.md`.**
- No Australian precedent found of a takedown demand against someone republishing FOI documents (searched, nothing found; UNVERIFIED either way).
